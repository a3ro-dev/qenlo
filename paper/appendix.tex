\section{Failure record}
Three pod/image attempts failed to provide usable WGPU: a PyTorch image exposed CUDA but Vulkan selected software or could not load the NVIDIA ICD; NVIDIA Vulkan images either lacked SSH or used an obsolete unsigned package repository. A 5.56 GiB public dataset transfer was aborted after throughput made it economically inappropriate. cuVS retained attempts failed or returned recall 0.9. These cells were not replaced with successful samples.
