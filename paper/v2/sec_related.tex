\section{Related work}\label{sec:related}
\paragraph{Power management versus latency.} On servers, low-power states and slow frequency governors are known to inflate the latency of latency-critical services at low load \citep{meisner2011powermgmt,kanev2014tradeoffs}, motivating latency-aware controllers \citep{lo2014pegasus,kasture2015rubik,hsu2015adrenaline,chou2019mudpm}. Mobile GPU governors mispredict bursty and concurrent work \citep{pathania2014integrated,lin2023geardvfs,zhang2025mobiledvfs}, and at low locked clocks desktop GPUs can drop into idle-like states \citep{adamek2021fftedge}. GPU DVFS work for machine learning treats frequency as a knob to \emph{lower} for energy \citep{tang2019gpudvfsdl,you2023zeus,chung2024perseus,qiu2024muserve,stojkovic2025dynamollm,kakolyris2025throttllem,maliakel2025llmdvfs,spaan2026kerneldvfs}. We study the opposite situation: the default policy under-clocks latency-bound work that the application would gladly run fast. We are not aware of a report that this reverses the ranking of two implementations of the same GPU operation, or that an unrelated co-running GPU load speeds a latency-bound GPU workload up.

\paragraph{Benchmark validity.} Innocuous setup details bias measured speed-ups \citep{mytkowicz2009wrongdata}; variation across executions must be sampled and reported \citep{kalibera2013rigorous,maricq2018taming}. For GPUs, power management causes 8--22\% variation across same-model devices under load \citep{sinha2022notallgpus} and 1--14\% in GEMM timings \citep{golden2025prism}; kernel benchmarking practice therefore often locks clocks \citep{oliaro2026fastkernels,chu2026operatorprofiler}, and \texttt{nvidia-smi} telemetry itself is sampled only part-time \citep{yang2023parttime}. Our effect is an order of magnitude larger than these variations and has a sign that favors the less efficient implementation.

\paragraph{Filtered vector search and CPU/GPU placement.} Filtered nearest-neighbor search is an active area \citep{gollapudi2023filtered,patel2024acorn,iff2026fanns,shi2025unified,li2025attribute,amanbayev2026systems}, including GPU systems \citep{johnson2019billion,xi2025vecflow}. Whether to run vector search on a GPU at all depends on data movement and batch size \citep{gregg2011whereisdata,mageirakos2026togpu}, and cost-model-based CPU/GPU operator placement is known to be fragile \citep{bress2016robust}. The choice between an eligible-id list and a full-width filter mirrors selection vectors versus bitmaps in vectorized databases \citep{ngom2021filter}. Our contribution to this line is that the measured margins between such alternatives depend on the GPU's power state, which the alternatives themselves influence.
