Current research · GPU power states

The efficient kernel runs slow.

Our compact-row GPU path varied up to 4.6× across tested fresh processes and hosts, while a heavier path was more stable. A load-raising experiment reduced the device-time tail, but its predicted end-to-end speedup failed. We publish both results and the protocols behind them.

Read the paper Inspect the evidence
Scientific Integrity & Evidence Status: Qenlo is a research-grade alpha project licensed under Apache 2.0. The numbers above are device timestamps from a five-host RTX 4090 power-state study (not end-to-end latency), a preregistered held-out gate that a candidate CPU/GPU router failed and stayed reverted, and a SHA-256 inventory of retained evidence files regenerated and diffed in CI — none is a universal production guarantee.
