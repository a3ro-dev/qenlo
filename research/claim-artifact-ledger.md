## Pipeline defects that block a clean-clone paper claim

1. Android evidence remains author-supplied aggregate data; raw-call regeneration is impossible.
2. Bootstrap-comparison JSON files containing the original endpoint confidence intervals are retained but not recomputed by the top-level paper command.
3. Appendix tables are typeset in LaTeX rather than generated fragments, so release review must check them against processed CSVs.
4. The external dataset preparation record is sufficient for the retained native corpus but not a universal recipe for every contextual library cohort.

These limitations prevent a claim of one-command regeneration of every contextual
number. They do not break the direct raw-evidence path for the headline native
crossover, static-policy counterfactual, or eligibility-materialization ablation.
