## Pipeline defects that block a clean-clone paper claim

1. `analyze_results.py` says it recomputes every processed table, but it does not generate `native_crossover_summary.csv`, `android_device_lab.csv`, `retained_evidence.csv`, or `evidence_completeness.csv`.
2. Appendix tables are handwritten instead of generated or checked against processed data.
3. Bootstrap-comparison JSON files containing the headline confidence intervals are not regenerated by `paper/REPRODUCE.md`.
4. PDF metadata prevents byte-identical figure regeneration; the underlying PNG/data outputs did reproduce.
5. The paper points to a commit plus uncommitted changes rather than one immutable source revision.
6. The data preparation record does not yet provide a complete clean-clone download/checksum/preparation path for every external dataset.

Until these defects are fixed, claims with retained raw evidence can be audited manually but the paper cannot truthfully claim one-command, clean-clone regeneration of every number, table, and figure.
