## Q3. Which code generated each experiment's data

### Takeaway

Laptop runs record e5d85ad (D1), 99a92c9 (E-LOCK) and 236b8d4 (E-SPLIT), all with `git_worktree_dirty=true`. The protocol argues these don't affect the measured kernels.

## Q7. Exclusions and replacements vs the logs

### Takeaway
The raw data and logs agree with the paper's account of the block-3 replacement, the block-4 stray setup process, and the E-SPLIT memory exclusions. Two nuances: the "14× slower" figure comes from a Python micro-benchmark (5.9 s vs 0.408 s), while bench wall times were about 10× (paper says both, consistently), and the replacement pod was chosen after the slow pod's partial results already existed on disk (the decision note says it was made "for host speed before looking at any b3 results").
